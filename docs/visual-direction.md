# Visual Direction — Lumen Building

## Tokens

- Porcelain `#F4F6F8`: wizard workspace
- Midnight `#09111F`: presentation environment
- Signal cyan `#38D9F5`: selected systems and interaction
- Warm interior `#FFCB8A`: occupied rooms and architectural warmth
- Structural silver `#B7C1CC`: frames, fins, and equipment
- Status red `#FF5D62`: critical incidents only

Typography uses IBM Plex Sans Thai for interface clarity and Manrope for large presentation statements. The building fills the frame; interface chrome remains quiet and aligned to its floor lines.

## Composition

The memorable element is a faceted glass office tower with stacked sky gardens and a warm cutaway interior. The opening camera sits below the roofline so the tower feels architectural rather than like a dashboard illustration. Guided view buttons move the camera between exterior, cutaway, and plant-room story beats.

The design avoids generic KPI-card grids and decorative gradients. Data is attached to physical equipment and floors. Cyan identifies the active system; warm light communicates occupied space.
