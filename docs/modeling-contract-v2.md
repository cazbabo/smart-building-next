# Premium Office Tower — Modeling Contract v2

- Purpose: executive Smart Building presentation on laptop/desktop.
- Finish: polished architectural visualization. Low-poly was not requested.
- Required forms: transparent double-height lobby, slender glass office tower, vertical aluminium fins, two planted sky terraces, meeting-floor reveal, rooftop solar canopy and screened mechanical plant.
- Materials: low-iron blue glass, porcelain concrete, dark anodized aluminium, warm occupied interiors and restrained cyan system light.
- Spatial relationships: every upper floor sits on a visible slab; facade fins attach to slab edges; canopy has columns; planters sit on terraces; solar panels sit on roof frames.
- Runtime target: smooth interaction on a representative laptop; repeated facade elements use shared geometry and materials.
- Review views: opening perspective, front elevation, cutaway office, rooftop energy and B2 plant close-up.
- Perceptual gates: silhouette must read as a contemporary premium office, window rhythm must be deliberate rather than random, edges cannot read as an unfinished blockout, and the building must remain legible against the UI.
- Typography gate: Thai interface text at least 13px for navigation/body, strong contrast, regular-to-semibold weights, and no essential information in faint microtype.
